Patrick Layormie Taylor-Ayikon
Patrick Layormie Taylor-Ayikon brings a wealth of operational and strategic expertise to African Projects Ventures Investments and Advancement Limited (APVIA Ltd), drawing on an extensive career spanning marine engineering, logistics, shipping, and diplomatic engagement across West Africa. His deep industry knowledge and established relationships make him an invaluable asset to the company's leadership team.
Marine Engineering Foundation
A trained marine engineer by profession, Patrick has accumulated over 18 years of hands-on experience across various marine engineering and fishing vessel positions. This technical background provides him with a unique operational perspective, enabling him to understand complex engineering challenges, maritime regulations, and the practical realities of project execution in coastal and offshore environments.
Entrepreneurship & Logistics Leadership
In 2006, Patrick founded Tayman Business Agency, a company specialising in logistics and supply chain solutions. Under his leadership, the agency has developed extensive expertise in:
• Freight Management: End-to-end freight coordination for prominent organisations across West Africa
• Shipping Operations: Comprehensive shipping services and vessel coordination
• Clearing Services: Efficient customs clearing and regulatory compliance
• Supply Chain Solutions: Integrated logistics and supply chain facilitation
Through Tayman Business Agency, Patrick has built a reputation for delivering reliable, efficient, and cost-effective logistics solutions to a diverse portfolio of clients throughout the region.
Diplomatic & Protocol Expertise
Patrick possesses extensive experience in diplomatic protocols, having supported international organisations and senior government officials from various African countries. His understanding of diplomatic etiquette, cross-cultural engagement, and high-level coordination has been instrumental in facilitating successful international partnerships and official engagements.
Government & Institutional Relationships
Patrick maintains strong and well-established affiliations with all governing bodies within the Government of Sierra Leone. He has cultivated robust working relationships with:

These connections, built over years of professional engagement, enable APVIA Ltd to navigate the Sierra Leonean business landscape effectively, ensuring regulatory compliance, facilitating project approvals, and fostering productive partnerships with the public sector.
