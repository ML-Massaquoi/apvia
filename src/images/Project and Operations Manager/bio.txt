Professional Summary
Ibrahim Rogers is a professional Geologist, with a stellar reputation in the Mineral Sands Industry. As a Geologist with consistently over a decade and half of work experience in the Mineral Sands ecosystem, he was responsible for identifying potential mineral resource areas in different deposits; firstly, at Sierra Rutile Limited (a mineral sands company operating world-class assets and producing high quality rutile, ilmenite and zircon) and as Director of Operations at Harmony Minerals (SL)Ltd (a multi-elements exploration company with strategic focus on lithium, coltan and heavy mineral sands).
At Sierra Rutile Limited, he was very strategic and a key driver in the evaluation of both green and brown field mineral sand assets. 
Being the team leader providing strategic directions, Ibrahim was part of a team that made discovery of two contiguous mineral sand deposits which were previously considered sterile. 
During these years, as part of these companies strategic objectives, Ibrahim supported in providing a more sustainable cost base; through effective planning, resourcing and ensuring the implementation and management of all exploration activities over target areas in a timely and efficient manner. 
Before those excitement moments, Ibrahim graduated with a BSc honours (First-Class) in Geology at Fourah Bay College – University of Sierra Leone. As a professional who believes in sustained competitive personal development, he proceeded to  acquire essential knowledge on mineral resources management, exploration and mining geology through various institutions such as Jiangxi College (China) and Edumine (through University of British Columbia – Canada) respectively. He is currently enrolled at the Unicaf University, pursuing a Master’s degree in Business Administration, whilst also pursuing a certification program on Financial Modeling and Valuation Analysis (FMVA) with the Corporate Finance Institute based in the United States.
He is currently a member and Director of the Executive Committee of a professional body in Sierra Leone called the Sierra Leone Institution of Geocientists (SLIG).
