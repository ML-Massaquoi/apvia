Vincent Davies is a seasoned Human Resources and Administration professional with over 16 years of progressive experience across the extractive, fintech and aviation industries. His diverse sector experience has given him a strong understanding of the people, operational and regulatory dynamics that shape organisations operating in complex and highly regulated environments.

Throughout his career, Vincent has developed expertise in human capital management, employee relations, talent acquisition and development, organisational development, performance management, workforce planning, HR policy development and employee engagement. His experience across the extractive sector, fintech and aviation has strengthened his ability to adapt HR strategies to different business environments while maintaining a strong focus on operational efficiency, compliance and organisational performance.

As Human Resources & Administration Manager at APVIA Limited, Vincent provides strategic leadership in building the company’s human resources and administrative framework. He is responsible for developing people-centred policies, systems and practices that support APVIA’s growth, while ensuring that the organisation attracts, develops and retains the talent required to deliver its strategic objectives.

Vincent brings a practical, people-focused and solutions-oriented approach to HR leadership. He has experience managing complex employee-relations matters, strengthening performance management systems, supporting organisational change, developing HR policies and procedures, and aligning workforce capabilities with broader business objectives. His approach is grounded in professionalism, fairness, accountability and continuous improvement.

His cross-sector experience is particularly valuable to APVIA as the company develops and pursues opportunities across infrastructure, investment, logistics, industrial development and other emerging business sectors. Vincent is committed to building a strong organisational culture and developing professional, capable and high-performing teams that can support APVIA’s long-term growth.

Vincent holds a Bachelor of Arts in English Language/Sociology from the University of Sierra Leone and is currently pursuing a Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Nexford University. He also holds professional certificates in Performance Management and Job Management from Premier HR and is a member of the Institute of HR Professionals Sierra Leone.

At APVIA Limited, Vincent plays a strategic role in ensuring that the company’s most important asset—its people—is effectively managed, developed and positioned to drive operational excellence, innovation and sustainable business growth.
